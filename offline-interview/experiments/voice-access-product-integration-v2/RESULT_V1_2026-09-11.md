# Voice Access product integration v1 — field result 2026-09-11

Environment: Android / Edge 152 (`Linux armv81`).

Observed:

- master audio intact: `trackMuted=false`, `trackEnded=false`, `recorderError=false`, 1,673,553 bytes, human `audioContinuous=true`;
- Voice Access committed text while MediaRecorder was `recording` on T1, T2, T3, T5, T6, T7 and T8;
- speaker change was observed;
- one input on T3 occurred 704 ms after transition;
- no technical `routingAnomalies` were recorded by v1, but human `routingCorrect=false` and `exportCoherent=false`;
- mandatory T4 keyboard+human-lock scenario was not completed;
- mandatory T6 audio-only scenario was not completed;
- controlled back-navigation scenario was not completed;
- v1 verdict: `FAIL_TEXT_ROUTING`.

Interpretation:

This is **not** evidence that Voice Access cannot support product integration. It is evidence that the v1 harness mixed product-risk observation with an insufficiently constrained human protocol. In particular, v1 treated any input within 1200 ms of a transition as a late mutation even when it could be a legitimate new-turn response, while required scenarios could be skipped entirely.

Decision: build v2 with enforced scenario gates, physical hold navigation and an explicit no-focus transition quarantine. Qualified beta/runtime remains unchanged.
