## Interpretation

This closes the timing uncertainty from V1: on this tested Android/Edge environment, Voice Access can commit text into a focused Web field while the browser master audio recorder is still recording, without observed damage to the audio capture path.

This is still a single-device/browser candidate result, not a universal Android guarantee. The next qualification target is a product-like multi-turn interview with a single continuous master recording and per-turn text routing.
